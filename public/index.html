What's inside

Quests

Post a quest with a category, difficulty, objectives and the proof you need. Claims expire on their own, so nothing stays stuck.

XP and levels

Easy, Medium and Hard quests give 10, 30 and 60 XP. Six levels, from Apprentice to Legend.

Questions

Is it free? +

Yes. Quest Board is free to use. You host it yourself, so there is no subscription.

Is it a replacement for Jira or Trello? +

No. It is for the small, useful tasks around the main work: docs, tooling, research and experiments. Keep client work in your project tool.

Can people sign themselves up? +

No. There is no sign-up page. An admin adds members and picks their portraits, and each team runs its own install.

What do I need to run it? +

A server with Docker Compose. It runs four containers: the app, a queue worker, a scheduler and MySQL.

Can I change how it looks? +

Yes. Admins change the name, tagline, logo, banner, favicon, mascot and color theme in Settings > Branding. Four dark themes are built in.

Is there a light mode? +

No. Quest Board is dark only, on purpose. Animations respect your reduced-motion setting.

What does the demo show? +

A shared demo team with sample quests. Anything you change is reset every night, so do not post anything private.
